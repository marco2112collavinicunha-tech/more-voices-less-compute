## Corrections to entries above (15 Oct 2026, after the independent audit)

- **17:10 entries, "before any accuracy? yes"** is wrong as written. Speech Commands frozen-classifier accuracy had been computed at 16:21–16:30 (RUNLOG_mac.txt lines 434–450) and Whisper-TORGO had written 178 outputs (cap 200); neither had been opened. Correct reading: *before any accuracy was inspected*.
- **19:05 entry**: TORGO frozen and shared accuracy had been printed to the run log at 18:37 by stats-torgo (RUNLOG_mac.txt lines 1142–1205). The log was only searched for step headers and errors, not read; the output was not inspected.
- **"forced choice over up to 95 candidates"**: s06 scored every item the speaker produced (164–595 candidates per recording, e.g. FC02 = 595), and s08 then restricted the decision to the speaker's eligible items (11–95). The time per recording was driven by the larger number. Costs in the report are for the eligible items, which is what the decision needs.
- **Fine-tuning energy**: no energy meter ran on the Mac (energy_meter = none in results/s07). Fine-tuning cost is reported in FLOPs only; wall-clock times are inflated by GPU sharing with Whisper and by memory swapping (27–31 GB of swap in use, RUNLOG_mac4/5/6), and are not used as cost.
- **Fine-tuning representation**: full, top-4 and LoRA read the last hidden state (layer 24 plus the final LayerNorm), as the pre-registered code did from the start; full_L12 reads layer 12. Not a change; recorded because frozen layer-24 accuracy turned out to collapse.
- **Amendments**: config/study.json carries three amendment blocks: 14 Oct morning (before any accuracy existed), 14 Oct 17:10 (after Speech Commands accuracy existed, before any was inspected), 14 Oct 19:05 (after TORGO frozen accuracy had been printed, not inspected). The report must say so.
- **08:15 entry (s08b)**: its reproduction counts were written before the final s08b run; the current results/s08b/torgo_matched.json has 96 checks, 93 exact (all 1-NN checks exact; three shared-classifier checks differ by up to 1.1 points). The report quotes the results file.
